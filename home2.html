Our Specialties

Comprehensive Cardiac Specializations

Our multidisciplinary team covers every aspect of cardiovascular care, from prevention to complex interventions.

Interventional Cardiology

Minimally invasive catheter-based procedures for coronary artery disease and structural heart conditions.

Electrophysiology

Advanced diagnosis and treatment of heart rhythm disorders including ablation and pacemaker implantation.

Preventive Cardiology

Risk assessment, lifestyle modification, and early detection programs to prevent cardiovascular disease.

Cardiac Rehabilitation

Supervised exercise programs and lifestyle counseling to aid recovery after cardiac events or surgery.

Vascular Surgery

Surgical and endovascular treatment of conditions affecting blood vessels throughout the body.

Heart Failure Management

Comprehensive care programs for chronic heart failure, including advanced device therapies and transplant evaluation.

Your Journey

The Patient Experience

From your first call to long-term follow-up, here's what your CardioVita care journey looks like.

1

Initial Consultation

Meet with one of our specialists for a thorough evaluation of your cardiac health, medical history, and risk factors. We listen carefully to understand your concerns.

2

Diagnostic Testing

Based on your consultation, we may recommend advanced diagnostics such as ECG, echocardiogram, stress test, or cardiac MRI for a complete picture of your heart health.

3

Treatment Planning

Your cardiologist develops a personalized treatment plan — whether it's lifestyle modifications, medication management, or an interventional procedure — tailored to your specific needs.

4

Recovery & Follow-Up

Ongoing monitoring, cardiac rehabilitation, and regular follow-up appointments ensure your continued progress and long-term heart health.
