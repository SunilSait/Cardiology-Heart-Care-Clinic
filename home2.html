Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers on appointment preparation, diagnostic procedures, telemetry monitoring, and accepted insurance plans.

Please bring a complete list of your current prescription medications, recent lab reports or ECG records, and your insurance card. We recommend wearing comfortable two-piece clothing, as a baseline 12-lead resting ECG and vital evaluation are performed during your initial examination.
An ECG records the electrical conduction and rhythm signals of your heart using skin sensors. An Echocardiogram uses ultrasound soundwaves to create high-resolution real-time video imaging of your heart valves, wall motion, and blood ejection fraction. Both are non-invasive and painless.
Yes. CardioVita is in-network with all major national health insurance providers, including Medicare, BlueCross BlueShield, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare. Our patient financial advocates handle prior-authorization paperwork to ensure transparent coverage.
If you experience crushing chest pain radiating to your jaw or left arm, severe shortness of breath, or sudden loss of consciousness, call 911 immediately. For new-onset fluttering palpitations, mild exertional shortness of breath, or borderline blood pressure spikes, our Same-Day Urgent Clinic provides rapid on-site diagnosis.
Our digital patch monitors transmit telemetry data in real-time via cellular gateway to our diagnostic reading hub. Complete physician-verified diagnostic summaries are uploaded to your Patient Portal within 24 to 48 hours following device turn-in.
