FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our cardiology services, appointments, and patient care.

You should see a cardiologist if you experience chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness, irregular heartbeat, persistent fatigue, swelling in legs or ankles, or if you have risk factors like high blood pressure, diabetes, or a family history of heart disease.

We offer comprehensive services including ECG monitoring, echocardiography, cardiac catheterization, stress testing, electrophysiology studies, nuclear cardiology, preventive screenings, cardiac rehabilitation, and emergency cardiac care.

Yes, we accept most major health insurance plans including Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, and Medicare. Please contact our billing team to verify your specific coverage before your visit.

You can schedule an appointment by calling +1 (800) 432-7428, using our online booking system through the patient dashboard, or visiting our contact page. We offer both in-person and telehealth consultations.

If you or someone else is experiencing a cardiac emergency such as severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, or loss of consciousness, call 911 immediately. Our emergency cardiac unit is available 24/7 for urgent cases.
