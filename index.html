Immediate Emergency

Heart Attack Warning Signs

Call 911 or our Emergency Hotline immediately. Every second of delay impacts cardiac muscle preservation.

  • Crushing central chest heaviness, pressure, or tightness
  • Radiating numbness or pain to left arm, neck, back, or jaw
  • Cold sweats, sudden dizziness, nausea, or impending doom
Emergency: (555) 911-HEART
Same-Day Walk-In

Acute Symptom Evaluation

For urgent new-onset symptoms that require same-day physician evaluation and rhythm diagnostics.

  • Rapid palpitations or fluttering heartbeat lasting > 15 mins
  • Shortness of breath climbing stairs or when resting flat
  • Unexplained bilateral fluid retention or ankle swelling
