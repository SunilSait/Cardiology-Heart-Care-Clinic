FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our pricing, insurance, and booking process.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), HSA/FSA cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers. Payment plans are also available for the Comprehensive and Executive plans — speak to our billing team for details.

We partner with most major insurance providers including Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, and Medicare. We recommend contacting our front desk or your insurance provider to verify your specific coverage and benefits before your visit.

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee on all plans. If you need to cancel, please notify us at least 48 hours before your scheduled appointment for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a 15% administrative fee. Completed services are refundable only under our satisfaction guarantee.

Each plan includes the specific tests, consultations, and follow-ups listed on the plan card and comparison table above. All plans include a comprehensive written report delivered within 48 hours. The Comprehensive and Executive tiers add advanced imaging, additional follow-ups, and priority access to our care team.

You can book online through our website by clicking "Get Started" or "Choose Plan" on any pricing card, or by visiting our Booking page. You can also call us directly at (555) 123-4567. Our team is available Monday to Saturday, 8 AM – 6 PM to assist with scheduling.
