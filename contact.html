Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about visiting CardioVita Heart Care.

You can cancel or reschedule your appointment through your patient dashboard, by calling our front desk at +1 (800) 432-7428, or by emailing appointments@cardiovita.com. We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice so that we can offer the slot to another patient. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a small fee.

Our billing team can verify your insurance before your visit. Simply call +1 (800) 432-7428 and select option 3 for Billing & Insurance, or submit your insurance details through the patient portal. We accept most major insurance plans including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, and Medicare. If your plan is not listed, contact us and we'll confirm coverage for you.

For your first appointment, please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications (including dosages), any relevant medical records or test results from your referring physician, and a list of questions you'd like to discuss. We recommend arriving 15 minutes early to complete the registration process. You can also fill out the new patient forms online through your patient portal ahead of time.

Heart Emergency?

If you or someone near you is experiencing chest pain, shortness of breath, sudden dizziness, or other signs of a cardiac emergency — do not wait. Every second counts when it comes to heart health. Call 911 immediately or visit your nearest emergency room. Our emergency cardiac team is available 24/7 for life-threatening situations.
